About Sahaay

A final-year BCA project with a real mission: make access easier.

Our purpose

Sahaay (सहाय — "help" in Hindi) is an accessibility platform that brings essential services and opportunities to one place for people with disabilities. Finding an inclusive job, an accessible doctor, or a place to learn sign language usually means searching many different websites — if they exist at all. Sahaay bundles all of it behind one simple, accessible interface.

Who Sahaay is for

  • People with disabilities — job seekers, patients and learners who need services to work for them.
  • Employers — companies committed to inclusive hiring, who can post jobs with explicit accommodations and review applications.
  • Doctors & healthcare providers — who offer accessible care and want to be discovered.
  • Families, caregivers & allies — who support people with disabilities every day.

The problem we solve

Accessibility isn't only about ramps and screen readers. It is about information being reachable: knowing which companies provide accommodations, which clinics are wheelchair accessible, and where to learn sign language. Sahaay makes that information first-class data — stored in a real database, searchable, and connected to working features like applications, appointments and recommendations.

How it's built

Sahaay is a complete full-stack application: a React frontend, a Flask (Python) REST API, a PostgreSQL database, and an AI/ML layer with a natural-language assistant and a content-based job recommendation engine (TF-IDF + cosine similarity). Every button works — login, job applications, community posts, appointments, admin management — nothing is mocked.

🔐 Role-based access

Sahaay has four account types. Users apply for jobs, book appointments and post in the community. Employers post jobs and review applications. Doctors manage their clinic profiles. Admins manage users, job approvals, doctors, sign-language content and community reports — all from a live statistics dashboard.
